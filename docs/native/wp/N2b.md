# N2b · Integration: runtime, renderer and app

The native executable `mh4u-native` (D8, D12) runs the game on the native runtime of N1, draws it with the renderer of N2a, shows it in a window of its own through a compositor with the app's screen layouts, and puts the app's ImGui UI over it. The phase ends when **G1–G4 pass end to end, images included, and G3's fight runs at 120 fps on the reference PC within the budget of [`../ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) §7** ([`LATER.md`](LATER.md), ROADMAP §5). The workflow is [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md); the rules for code in `native/` are at the top of [`N1.md`](N1.md) and the shared rules (formats, Old 3DS runs, *(CI)* items, needs tags, the two ways of comparing) at the top of [`N0.md`](N0.md). They apply here, with the rules below.

**Where things stand (2026-10-22), and what N2b is written against.** N0's goldens G1, G2 and G6 are recorded (N0-09); G3, G4 and G3-perf are (N0-10, N0-35), and so is G8 (N0-35); G7 was dropped (D91). N1-21 (the first game boot) is being done by the local session; N1-26, N1-25 and N1-23 follow it. Of N2a, the capture player, the GX engine, the PICA command processor, textures and the HD pre-processor are done (N2-02, N2-03, N2-04, N2-14, N2-17); everything else, and D4 (the RHI) and D5 (the fragment pipeline), are open (D4 has since been decided, on 2026-10-23: option 2b, and N2-21 split into N2-25 to N2-30; the bullet below uses their names). So the WPs below are written against the contracts the N1 and N2a specs promise, not against code that exists:
- **The backend (N2-25 to N2-29; D4: plume's Vulkan backend, patched, under an operation-level wrapper of ours; its owner-rule checks are on in every build type unless `MH4N_RHI_CHECKS` is off, N2-26):** the renderer-facing `native/src/gpu/backend/rhi.h` and the Vulkan side `vulkan_access.h`, both frozen by N2-26. N2-26: `CreateInstance` apart from `CreateBackend` (an optional surface; with one, `VK_KHR_swapchain`, a queue that can present, and `VK_KHR_present_id` with `VK_KHR_present_wait` when the device offers them, reported by `PresentWaitAvailable()`, with `PresentWaitMeasuresDisplay()` and `DisplayTimingAvailable()` beside it); a timeline value returned by each submission; one submit lock per queue, shared by the renderer thread and the present thread (`QueueLock` for a present); the Vulkan handles (instance, physical device, device, queue family, queue) and the `VkCommandBuffer` being recorded, readable; the capability structure and its mask (`MH4N_RHI_MASK`); the test helper `native/tests/support/vulkan_device.h` (a test that needs a device skips without one and fails under `MH4N_REQUIRE_VULKAN=1`, which CI sets on its Linux job; `MakeHeadlessSurface`). N2-27: images and render targets, `WrapExternalImage` and `PrepareForPresent` for a swapchain image, the one layout (`GENERAL`) and the owner rule. N2-28: waits on another submitter's value with their own stage, readback into guest memory, `ReadImage(image, x, y, width, height)` (a region of any backend image to the host in its own format), timestamps.
- **The rest of N2a:** `--gpu native`, the renderer assembled (N2-08, `native/src/gpu/native/`); the GPU wrapper `queue` (N2-22: a renderer thread, interrupts in completion order, `VBlank` queued in order with the commands, `ReadReg` answered at once from a mirror, at most one frame in flight, the thread's busy and wait times as `TakeFrameCounters`); the surface cache with `--scale N` and write-back on demand (N2-15); the pipeline cache in the user folder (N2-16); HD streaming with `--hd ARCHIVE` and a VRAM budget (N2-18); glslang vendored by N2-06 (`native/third_party/glslang`); `GpuDebug::TakeFrameCounters` and `TakeFrameGpuTimeNs` (N2-02).
- **N1:** the game boots and passes G1 and G2 headless (N1-21) and G3 (N1-26); the deterministic mode (N1-25); `--perf-log` with the game thread's time per frame and its verdict line (N1-23); `NativePlatform::Find(rt)` with `TopScreenRows`, `TopScreenWidth`, `Overlay`, `LayoutRefreshes` and `RequestedRefreshRate` (N1-20, `native/src/platform/platform_native.h`); `Runtime::SetRefreshRate` callable from any thread (D44).
- **The app:** `IRuntime` (`port/app/runtime.h`, N0-20, N0-24), still with the transitional `HostSystem()` and a `Window&` that is Azahar's `EmuWindow`, and the UI (`port/app/ui/`) still written against SDL2 and that window.

When an N2a or N1 WP merges with names other than these, the planner revises the *Spec* sections that use them in one short pass (as N2a does for D4 and D5); the goals, oracles and *Done when* lists stand.

## Rules for this phase

These add to the rules at the top of `N1.md` and `N0.md`.
1. **Two executables, one UI** (D12, proposed D55). The app's shared sources stay in `port/app/` and compile into both `mh4u` (Azahar, SDL2) and `mh4u-native` (SDL3). After N2b-02 the shared ones (the list `tests/test_native_app_portable.py` holds) include no Azahar header and no SDL header: the window, its events and the few host services they need come through `port/app/app_window.h` (`App::IWindow`, `App::InputEvent`, `App::Host`), which each executable implements. `multiplayer.cpp` and `launcher/` stay the Azahar app's (rooms are N4); `mh4u-native` has a stub of `App::Multiplayer` and `Launcher::Lobby` (N2b-03). A WP that changes a file under `port/app/` that `mh4u` compiles keeps the Azahar app green (`linux.yml`, `windows.yml` and `macos.yml` dispatched with `ref` set to its branch, *(CI)*) and passes the maintainer's smoke test of `docs/native/app-smoke.md` (`pc`); files `mh4u` does not compile are exempt.
2. **SDL3 and ImGui only in `native/app/`.** The modules under `native/src/` are linked into every binary (the runner, the tools, every test), so none of them includes SDL or ImGui; they take what they need from the app through plain interfaces (a `VkSurfaceKHR`, an input state, an opaque recorder). `native/app/` is its own tree (N2b-03): its modules link SDL3, ImGui, inih and the app's shared sources, and only `mh4u-native` and the tests under `native/app/tests/` link them. The executable's own options register in the `runner` scope from `native/app/` modules, so they never show in the runner's `--help`. The app build is the configure line of `N1.md` plus `-DMH4N_APP=ON` (default `OFF`), in its own build folder:
   ```
   source ~/.mh4n_env && cmake -S native -B build/native-app -G Ninja -DCMAKE_C_COMPILER=clang \
         -DCMAKE_CXX_COMPILER=clang++ -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=RelWithDebInfo -DMH4N_WERROR=ON -DMH4N_APP=ON
   cmake --build build/native-app
   ```
3. **Threads** (principles 4 and 6; proposed D56). `mh4u-native` has four host threads of its own: the **main** thread (SDL's events, the UI's frames, every `IRuntime` call), the **game** thread (`Runtime::Boot`: the scheduler and the guest fibers), the **renderer** thread (N2-22) and the **present** thread (N2b-06); plus the renderer's workers (pipeline compiles, HD streaming). Only the game thread touches guest memory, kernel objects and the hooks' state; the others hand work over with `Kernel::Post`, `gpu::Host::Post` or `Runtime::SetRefreshRate`, and read what the game thread publishes (atomics or a copy under a lock). **The game never waits for presentation:** the present thread takes the newest displayed frame from a mailbox and drops older ones. The main thread never blocks on the game thread, except `IRuntime::SetGameOption`'s bounded wait (N2b-25) and `IRuntime::Stop`, which joins it.
4. **One Vulkan device per process** (proposed D56). `mh4n/present.h` (N2b-26) holds the process's shared-backend slot; `mh4u-native` puts its backend there before the GPU is made (N2b-09), and the renderer's factory uses it when it is set (N2b-12), so the compositor samples the renderer's images directly. The headless runner leaves it empty, and the renderer makes its own.
5. **Headless first.** Every presentation feature has a form that runs without a display: the compositor draws into an offscreen image, `mh4u-native --headless --present-dump DIR` writes the composed window as PPM, SDL3's `offscreen` video driver makes windows and Vulkan surfaces through `VK_EXT_headless_surface` (as SDL 3.4.18's `src/video/offscreen/SDL_offscreenvulkan.c` does), and lavapipe runs it in the cloud and in Linux CI. What needs a real display (vsync, the monitor's refresh, the AMD driver, how it looks) is a maintainer procedure on the reference PC (`pc`).
6. **Checks that cannot pass silently.** Every ctest item, a whole-suite run included, is run with `--no-tests=error`, a single test as `ctest --test-dir <build folder> --no-tests=error -R '^<test>$' --output-on-failure` (without `--no-tests=error` ctest exits 0 when it finds no test). A test that needs a Vulkan device is run with `MH4N_REQUIRE_VULKAN=1` (it then fails, not skips, without one), and its log names the llvmpipe device in the cloud and in CI. A pytest item that can skip passes only with 0 skipped (`-rs` lists none), or sets the variable that turns its skip into a failure (`MH4N_REQUIRE_TEST_DATA=1`).
7. **The native golden check.** "The native check of S with ARGS, requiring R" means, on the cloud machine with the dump staged:
   ```
   python3 tools/native/golden.py check S --runner <the reference runner> --game baserom/juego.cxi \
       --update baserom/update_v130.cia --code <the reference code library> --out build/golden \
       --candidate-runner build/native/bin/mh4u_native_runner --candidate-program build/native-game \
       --candidate-code <the --native code library> --candidate-args "ARGS" --profile cross --require R
   ```
   (`--runner`, `--game` and the reference `--code` are required even with a candidate runner; the reference runner is the N0-29 one of `docs/native/golden.md`; `build/native-game` is `tools/native/prepare_game.py`'s folder.) It passes when it exits 0.
8. **Thresholds** are never changed here: images, ROADMAP §6 (PSNR ≥ 40 dB and no 16×16 block with a mean error above 8/255, the rule `native/src/gpu_capture/image.h` implements); scene timing across timing models, D50 (4 frames, a 60-frame input margin); the DSP capture, D51; `fs:` requests, D52; time, ROADMAP §7 and D19 (game thread ≤ 4 ms, translation ≤ 3 ms, GPU ≤ 6 ms at 3440×1440 with the HD pack, 120 fps with p99 ≤ 8.3 ms, no stutter over 50 ms). A check that cannot meet one stops the WP with `blocked` (or `needs-decision`, with the table), never with a looser number. **Every other number in this file** (the image-frame coverage of N2b-17, the pacer's bands of N2b-23) **is a first guess, confirmed by the maintainer's runs; if it proves wrong, the WP stops with a `needs-decision` table, and the worker does not retune it.**
9. **Images across timing models** follow the decision N2b-17 settles (proposed D58): in the `cross` profile the screenshots are gated only at calibrated frames, with the thresholds above; frames in which the native run drew gas are reported and not judged (Azahar draws no gas, N2a's preamble). G3-perf has no image gate (the hooks that widen its top screen run only on the recomp backend, so jit cannot calibrate it; N0-30 leaves it out too): its pictures are checked by `--display-check` and by the maintainer (N2b-20, N2b-22). The `same` profile stays exact.
10. **Maintainer procedures** (every `pc` item) are written as numbered steps, each with its expected result and about how long it takes; they say what he sends back and where (the CSVs and lines of notes the procedure names, in the batch PR or the chat: never screenshots, captures or anything else of the game), and what to do when a step fails (stop, send the last 50 lines of the log the procedure names, and the step's number). The `pc` procedures of `mh4u-native` start with *Setting up mh4u-native on the reference PC* (`docs/native/app.md`, N2b-11).
11. **Game data.** Screenshots, present dumps, GPU logs and present logs of the game are game data: they go to `build/`, never into the repository, a test fixture or a report. Reports quote frame numbers, counts, PSNR values and times only. Fixtures are synthetic (our own 3DS programs, images made by the tests).
12. **Nothing the player sees changes by default without a decision:** the frame rate (the `fps` of `mh4u.ini`), the layouts, the HD pack's default, the window's behaviour and the executable the launcher starts are the maintainer's (see *Decisions*).

### N2b-01 · The app's UI without Azahar's window and system
- **Size / agent:** M · native-worker-high (builds the Azahar app)
- **Depends on:** N0-24 · **Needs:** ci, pc
- **Goal:** the last Azahar types leave the app's shared sources: the UI talks to an `App::IWindow` instead of Azahar's `EmuWindow`, `IRuntime` loses `HostSystem()`, and the UI's remaining uses of `port/src` code that sits on Azahar (net sync, the options file) go through `IRuntime`. The Azahar app behaves exactly as before.
- **Read first:** `port/app/runtime.h` and the *As built* notes of N0-20 and N0-24 in [`N0.md`](N0.md); `port/app/window.h`, `window.cpp`; `port/app/ui/ui.cpp`, `ui.h`, `layer.h`, `menu.cpp`, `hint.cpp`; `port/app/launcher/screen_settings.cpp`; `port/app/main.cpp`; `port/src/frontend.h` (`OptionsPath`, `OptionsTemplate`) and `port/src/netsync.h` (`EndSkipPending`); `docs/native/azahar-deps.md`; `tests/test_native_app_util.py`.
- **Files (allowlist):** `port/app/app_window.h` (new), `port/app/runtime.h`, `port/app/runtime_azahar/**`, `port/app/window.h`, `port/app/{window.cpp,window_gl.cpp,window_vk.cpp,window_sw.cpp,main.cpp,multiplayer.cpp,multiplayer.h,CMakeLists.txt}`, `port/app/ui/{ui.cpp,ui.h,layer.h,menu.cpp,hint.cpp}`, `port/app/launcher/screen_settings.cpp`, `port/app/util/tests/**`, `tests/test_native_app_util.py`, `port/src/options_template.h` (new), `port/src/options_template.cpp` (new), `port/src/frontend.cpp`, `port/CMakeLists.txt`, `docs/native/azahar-deps.md`, `docs/native/app-smoke.md` (new).
- **Spec:**
  - **`App::IWindow`** (`port/app/app_window.h`), what the UI uses of a window today, and no more: `IsOpen`, `RequestClose`, `SetFullscreen(bool)`, `IsFullscreen`, `RequestInputReload`, `KeyboardMouseActive`, `ReleaseTouch` (the UI's `TouchReleased` call), `SizeInPoints` and `SizeInPixels` (each `{int w, h}`), and `SDL_Window* SdlWindow()` (an opaque pointer here; each executable's own SDL defines it). `App::Window` (Azahar's) implements it; `Ui::Layer`, `Ui::Start` and `menu.cpp` take `IWindow&` and no longer include `window.h`.
  - **`IRuntime`:** `HostSystem()` goes; `Load(const LoadRequest&, IWindow& window, IWindow* secondary)` (the Azahar runtime casts to `App::Window&`, which it made); new `std::vector<std::string> EndSkipPending()` (the Azahar runtime forwards to `Port::NetSync::EndSkipPending`, `hint.cpp` calls it). `Ui::Start` and `Multiplayer::Init` lose their unused `Core::System&`. `main.cpp` keeps the concrete `AzaharRuntime` and gets the system the windows need from it (a method of `AzaharRuntime`, not of `IRuntime`).
  - **The options file:** `Port::OptionsTemplate()`'s text moves to `port/src/options_template.{h,cpp}` (`Port::OptionsTemplateText()`, standard library only), which `frontend.cpp` calls; `screen_settings.cpp` uses `App::Runtime().GameOptionsPath()` instead of `Port::OptionsPath()` and no longer includes `frontend.h`.
  - The fake runtime of the app's tests (`port/app/util/tests/fake_runtime.h`) follows the interface.

### N2b-26 · The presentation contract (`mh4n/present.h`)
- **Size / agent:** S · native-worker-high (build-heavy)
- **Depends on:** N1-01 · **Needs:** none
- **Goal:** the contract by which a GPU hands its displayed screens to whoever shows them, and the process's shared-backend slot (rule 4), in one self-contained header, so the producers (N2b-07, N2b-13) and the consumers (N2b-05, N2b-06, N2b-14) can be built in parallel.
- **Read first:** [`../ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) §3 (principles 4 and 6); `native/include/mh4n/gpu.h` and `gpu_debug.h` (the style of a contract, `dynamic_cast` extensions); `native/src/gpu_capture/image.h` (`DisplayShadow::BuildScreen`: what a displayed screen is, its size and orientation).
- **Files (allowlist):** `native/include/mh4n/present.h` (new), `native/tests/present_contract_test.cpp`, `docs/native/presentation.md` (new).
- **Spec:** (`namespace mh4n::present`; the header includes only the standard library and `mh4n/` headers; the backend's objects appear as opaque `std::shared_ptr<const void>` or `void*`, which the producers and consumers, who both link the backend (N2-25 to N2-29), cast back)
  - `RgbImage { u32 width, height; std::vector<u8> rgb; }`: 3 bytes a pixel, top row first.
  - `ScreenImage`: either the backend's image (`std::shared_ptr<const void>`, kept alive while the frame is) with the region that holds the screen, the scale (image pixels per displayed pixel), the storage orientation (the 3DS's framebuffers are stored rotated by 90°) and the backend's timeline value of the submission that drew it (N2-26) (the compositor's submission waits on it); or CPU pixels (RGBA8, top row first, upright); in both cases the displayed size at 1x (top: 240 high and 400 wide, or the hooks' width; bottom: 320×240).
  - `DisplayFrame { u64 sequence; u32 game_frame; u64 tick; std::optional<ScreenImage> top, bottom; bool gas; }`: what a VBlank of the top screen shows (`nullopt`: that screen is off or not set up); `gas`: a draw since the previous frame used the gas mode (fog mode 7).
  - `ScreenSink { virtual void Publish(const DisplayFrame&) = 0; }`: thread-safe, returns at once.
  - `Display`, which a `gpu::Gpu` that can show its screens also implements (found with `dynamic_cast`): `AddScreenSink(ScreenSink*)`, `RemoveScreenSink(ScreenSink*)` (before and after the run), and `ReadScreen(const ScreenImage&, std::function<void(RgbImage)> done)`: the screen at 1x as RGB (downscaled from the internal scale with a box filter); `done` may run before `ReadScreen` returns (CPU pixels) or later on another thread.
  - `UiLayer { virtual void Record(void* recorder, u32 target_width, u32 target_height) = 0; }` (`recorder`: the backend's command recorder, opaque): called by the compositor after the screens, on the thread that composes.
  - **Which GPUs are display GPUs, before any is made:** `struct DisplayGpu {};` registered by name with `MH4N_REGISTER(present::DisplayGpu, "<the GpuFactory's name>", {})` from the module that registers that factory (N2b-07 for `null-display`, N2b-13 for `renderer`), and `bool IsDisplayGpu(std::string_view name)`: whether such a registration exists. A GPU registered so must implement `Display`. `mh4u-native` chooses its default `--gpu` with it (N2b-09) without making a GPU first.
  - **The shared-backend slot:** `SetSharedBackend(std::shared_ptr<void>)` and `std::shared_ptr<void> SharedBackend()` (thread-safe; empty by default): the backend object of the process (`rhi::Backend`, N2-26), set by `mh4u-native` before its GPU is made (N2b-09) and used by the renderer's factory when set (N2b-12).
  - **`docs/native/presentation.md`:** the contract, rule 4, and how a frame travels from VBlank to the window (N2b-05, N2b-06 and N2b-23 add their parts).
- **Done when:** `ctest --test-dir build/native --no-tests=error -R '^present_contract_test$' --output-on-failure` passes (no device needed): a `Display` found with `dynamic_cast` on a test GPU; `IsDisplayGpu` true for a name the test registers and false for another; sinks added and removed receive frames only in between; `ReadScreen` calling `done` synchronously and from another thread; the slot set and read from two threads.
- **Traps:** the header must compile with nothing but the standard library and `mh4n/` headers (no Vulkan, no backend header): the test includes it first.
- **Out of scope:** any implementation (N2b-05 on).

### N2b-06 · Swapchain and the present thread
- **Size / agent:** M · native-worker-high (build-heavy)
- **Depends on:** N2b-05 · **Needs:** none
- **Goal:** principle 4 for presentation: a present thread takes the newest `DisplayFrame` from a mailbox, composes it with the current layout and the UI layer, and presents it to a swapchain (or writes it to an offscreen target), handling vsync, resizes, minimising and shutdown, and logs when each frame was shown. Publishing a frame never blocks the renderer or the game.
- **Read first:** `docs/native/presentation.md` and `native/include/mh4n/present.h`; `native/src/present/` (N2b-05); the backend's `CreateBackend`, `PresentWaitAvailable()`, `PresentWaitMeasuresDisplay()`, `DisplayTimingAvailable()` and `vulkan_access.h` (the handles, `QueueLock`, `WrapExternalImage`), `PrepareForPresent` (`rhi.h`), and `MakeHeadlessSurface` in `native/tests/support/vulkan_device.h` (N2-26, N2-27); `docs/research/rhi-spike.md` 3.3, 7.3 and 9.4 items 5 and 9; D57; the Vulkan specification's chapters on `VK_KHR_swapchain`, present modes, `VK_KHR_present_id`/`VK_KHR_present_wait` and `VK_EXT_headless_surface`; how Azahar's Vulkan renderer handles an out-of-date swapchain (`src/video_core/renderer_vulkan/vk_swapchain.cpp`, `vk_present_window.cpp`).
- **Files (allowlist):** `native/src/swapchain/**` (new), `native/tests/swapchain_test.cpp`, `docs/native/presentation.md`.
- **Spec:**
  - **`swapchain::Presenter`**, made with the backend, a target (a `VkSurfaceKHR` the caller made on the backend's instance, or an offscreen size) and a `LayoutSource` (a callback the present thread calls per frame for the current `screen_layout::Inputs` and `ComposeOptions`):
    - is a `present::ScreenSink`: `Publish` stores the frame in a one-slot mailbox (a newer frame replaces an older one that was not shown, counted as dropped) and wakes the present thread;
    - `SetUiLayer(present::UiLayer*)`: the layer the compositor calls (N2b-11); `RequestRedraw()`: compose the last frame again (the UI changed while the game shows nothing new);
    - `CurrentLayout()`: the `screen_layout::Layout` of the last composed frame (thread-safe copy), for the shell's touch mapping (N2b-10 maps a click with N2b-04's `TouchTracker` on it and hands the point to the live source);
    - `SetVsync(bool)`: FIFO when on; when off, MAILBOX if the surface has it, else IMMEDIATE (Apple's surfaces offer no MAILBOX, `rhi-spike.md` X9: there it is IMMEDIATE);
    - `Resize(w, h)` and `SetMinimised(bool)`; the swapchain's own out-of-date and suboptimal answers recreate it on the present thread; while minimised (or at a zero extent) nothing is presented and the frames are counted as dropped (D60);
    - `Stop()`: the thread finishes the frame it has and exits; destruction waits for the device to be idle.
  - **Present log** (`SetPresentLog(path)`; the app's option, N2b-10): a CSV `# present v1 <date> refresh_hz=<the display's rate, if known>` header, then one row per presented frame: `sequence,game_frame,published_us,composed_us,presented_us,displayed_us,dropped_before`, host times from the steady clock. **Where `displayed_us` comes from** (D57: only displayed times count), the first source that applies: `VK_KHR_present_wait` when `PresentWaitMeasuresDisplay()` (not merely `PresentWaitAvailable()`: on MoltenVK the wait is released at command-buffer completion, not at display, `rhi-spike.md` 7.3); else `VK_GOOGLE_display_timing`'s past presentation times when `DisplayTimingAvailable()` (listed on MoltenVK, 3.3); else a `swapchain::DisplayTimeSource` (an interface of this module: given a presented frame's id and host present time, the time it was shown, or none) registered by name (`MH4N_REGISTER`): D57's second source, the operating system's presentation statistics on Windows, where the RX 6700 XT has neither extension (D57 has N2b-23 choose ETW or DWM with its measurement; N2b-23 implements that source and registers it); else empty: the time a frame is shown is not measurable (D57). The header line gains `display_time=<present_wait|display_timing|<source name>|none>`. At start the presenter logs once `present wait: on` or `present wait: off` (from `PresentWaitAvailable()`) and `display time: <source>`.
  - **Offscreen target** (`--present-dump`, N2b-10): `DumpTo(dir, every_n)` writes each n-th composed image and, at `Stop`, the last one, as `presented_<sequence>.ppm` and `last.ppm`.
  - **The owner rule** (`rhi-spike.md` 9.4 item 5): the present thread owns the swapchain images (wrapped with `WrapExternalImage`, N2-27) and its offscreen targets, records only on those, samples the renderer's images as N2b-05 says, submits in recording order, records `PrepareForPresent` on the swapchain image (N2-27: it moves the image to the present layout and keeps the layout plume holds true) as the last command of the frame's recorder, and presents with `vkQueuePresentKHR` under the backend's `QueueLock`, never around it (one queue is shared with the renderer thread, D56). With `MH4N_RHI_CHECKS` on (N2-26: on by default in every build type, `RelWithDebInfo` included), the presenter asserts that every recording, submission and present of its own happens on the present thread, calling the backend's owner-rule handler otherwise (the calls from other threads, `Publish`, `SetUiLayer`, `Resize` and the rest, only hand over state), and `swapchain_test` has a case where a recording forced onto another thread (a test hook) calls a test handler.
  - The tests' swapchain is on a `VK_EXT_headless_surface` surface made with the helper's `MakeHeadlessSurface` (N2-26).
  - The backend (N2-26) provides the surface extensions, a queue that can present, the submit lock and the handles; `presentation.md` gets the presenter's section.
- **Done when:** `MH4N_REQUIRE_VULKAN=1 ctest --test-dir build/native --no-tests=error -R '^swapchain_test$' --output-on-failure` passes on lavapipe (the log names the llvmpipe device): a `VK_EXT_headless_surface` swapchain presents frames in order; three frames published faster than they are presented show only the newest and count two dropped; `Publish` returns while a test hook holds the present thread inside a composition; a resize recreates the swapchain and the next frame has the new size; minimised presents nothing; `Stop` with a frame pending returns; the present log's rows match the frames shown; vsync off picks MAILBOX or IMMEDIATE as the surface offers; `CurrentLayout` returns the layout of the frame composed last.
- **Traps:** a swapchain's images belong to the presentation engine until acquired: never compose into one that was not acquired. One `VkQueue` is shared with the renderer thread: every submission goes through the submit lock, never around it.
- **Out of scope:** the window and its events (N2b-10); locking the VBlank to the display (N2b-23).

### N2b-08 · Live input, latched at frame boundaries
- **Size / agent:** S · native-worker-high (build-heavy; two threads share its state)
- **Depends on:** N1-16, N1-09 · **Needs:** none
- **Goal:** an input source the app can feed from any thread, which the game sees change only between its frames (proposed D59), so that any live session can be written down as a frame-keyed script and replayed on both runners.
- **Read first:** `native/include/mh4n/host.h` (`InputState`, `InputSource`, `InputSourceFactory`); `native/src/input_script/` (the script source); `port/src/schedule.h` and `schedule.cpp` (the script grammar: buttons, `TOUCH:x:y`, `CIRCLE:x:y`, holds in frames); `docs/native/runner.md`.

So a key pressed while the stylus is down never lifts it.
  - **Latching** (`--input-latch frame|none`, default `frame`): at each frame change (`Runtime::OnFrame`), the state set last becomes the one `Sample` returns until the next frame change; before the game's frame counter has been seen (and in programs that never map it) `Sample` returns the state set last.

- **Done when:** `ctest --test-dir build/native --no-tests=error -R '^input_live_test$' --output-on-failure` passes: states set between two frame changes are seen only from the second; with `--input-latch none` at once; a recorded sequence (buttons, touch, circle pad, overlapping holds) replayed through the script source gives the same `Sample` at every frame of a fake frame counter; a program without a frame counter is recorded in milliseconds; `Set` from one thread while `Sample` runs on another, 100 000 times, never yields a mixed state; a `Set` after a `SetTouch` keeps the touch; `runner.md` lists `--input-source live`, `--input-latch` and `--record-input`.
- **Traps:** `Set` runs on the main thread and `Sample` on the scheduler thread: no lock may be held across the frame callback; a seqlock or a mutex-guarded copy is enough.
- **Out of scope:** mapping keys and controllers to buttons (N2b-25 for the keyboard; N3-07 and N3-14 for the rest).

- **Goal:** the half of `App::IRuntime` that runs the game: `NativeRuntime` loads it on a game thread of its own, holds it until `Start`, shows it through the present thread on the process's one backend, and stops it cleanly; it exposes what the shell needs. The other methods are logged stubs until N2b-25.
- **Read first:** `port/app/runtime.h` (N2b-01, N2b-02) and `port/app/runtime_azahar/` (what each lifecycle method does there); `native/include/mh4n/runtime.h` (`Boot`, boot steps and their order, `RequestExit`, `OnFrame`); `native/include/mh4n/present.h`; `native/src/swapchain/` (N2b-06); `native/src/display_mem/`, `native/src/input_live/`; `port/app/util/log.h` (`SetLogSink`); `docs/native/runner.md` (the runner's options, exit statuses); D13, D43, D44.

The default `--gpu` is `renderer` when a `GpuFactory` of that name is registered and `present::IsDisplayGpu("renderer")` (N2b-26) is true, else `null-display`, with an Info line; nothing is made to find out; an explicit `--gpu` wins.
